External platform proof

Independent Host Platform (No Bootstrap, No Savanna CSS)

This page demonstrates that <db-assistant> operates as an isolated, standards-compliant web component inside any external LMS or learning portal without style bleeding or external dependencies.

1. Module Embedding Snippet

<!-- Load the standalone script --> <script src="https://your-platform/db-assistant.js" defer></script> <!-- Place the component --> <db-assistant lang="am" api="https://your-tutor-server" content="/content.json"></db-assistant> <!-- Listen to lifecycle events --> <script> document.addEventListener('db-assistant:question', (e) => console.log(e.detail)); document.addEventListener('db-assistant:escalated', (e) => console.log(e.detail)); </script>

Host lesson: writing a professional email

This lesson belongs to this host page, not to Savanna. The page sends it to the tutor with content="data/content.json", so Mimi can answer from it and link back here. Try asking Mimi: "How long should an email subject line be?"

A professional email starts with a clear subject line. Keep the subject line under eight words and say what you need, for example: Request: meeting on Tuesday. Avoid vague subject lines such as Hello or Question, because busy readers skip them.

End a professional email with one clear next step and a polite closing, such as Kind regards, followed by your full name. Reply to work emails within one working day, even if only to say when you will answer in full.
